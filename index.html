Why Pulsus Tech

Hidden exposure

FAQ

Frequently asked questions.

What does Pulsus Tech do?
It brings your applications, infrastructure, vendors, outages and contracts into one view, so leaders can see technology risk and its business impact before it becomes disruption.
Which questions does it answer for management?
  1. What is critical?
  2. What is underperforming?
  3. What is approaching end of support?
  4. Where are our concentrations and dependencies?
  5. Which third-party arrangements need attention?
  6. Where are the resilience and contractual gaps?
  7. And where should management act first?
Who is it for?
CIOs and CTOs, COOs, risk and compliance teams, and boards in any organisation where technology is critical: banks, credit unions, insurers, hospitals, telecoms, utilities and the public sector.
Do we have to replace our existing tools?
No. Pulsus Tech connects to ServiceNow, Datadog, ManageEngine, Freshservice and Zabbix, and takes a simple Excel workbook for anything else.
How quickly can we get started?
The same day. Sign up, add your business units, then upload the starter workbook or connect a system. Your dashboards fill in as soon as the figures arrive.
How does the free trial work?
You get 14 days with everything included and no card needed. When it ends, choose a plan to carry on; your figures and settings are kept.
How much does it cost?
US$500 per company per month, including 5 users. Each further user is US$50 a month, and people who only view are free. See Pricing.
Is our data secure?
Each company's data is kept separate and encrypted. Sign-in supports two-step verification and single sign-on, and every change is recorded with who made it and when.
Which languages does it support?
English and Spanish. Each person chooses their own.
Will it make us compliant with DORA or other regulations?
It gives you the evidence supervisors expect, assembled and current, but it does not certify compliance with any regulation.
Can we see it before signing up?
Yes. The live demo is the real product with example data, and needs no sign-up.
